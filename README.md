## 🎮 What You Get

**Zombie Runner** is a fully functional browser-based 3D wave-defense game featuring:

* 🔫 **5 unique weapons** — Pistol, Uzi, Shotgun, AK-47 & Laser
* 👾 **5 enemy types** — Dog, Skeleton, Goblin, Troll & Boss
* 📈 **Upgradeable stats** — Speed & Power, 5 levels each
* ⚡ **Special abilities** — Dash & Shield with cooldowns
* 🌊 **100 levels** of progressively increasing difficulty
* 🖥️ **Professional UI** with menus and settings
* 🌑 **3D graphics** with lighting and shadows
* 📦 **Completely offline playable**
* 🚫 **No installation required**
* 🚫 **No account required**

# 🎯 Strategy Guide

## 🟢 Early Game — Levels 1–10

Focus on building your economy.

**Recommended approach:**

* 🔫 Start with the Pistol
* 💰 Save your coins
* 🏃 Practice dodging enemies
* 🔥 Purchase the Uzi when affordable
* ⚡ Avoid unnecessary upgrades early

> **Goal:** Build enough coins to unlock stronger weapons.

---

## 🟡 Mid Game — Levels 11–50

Enemies become significantly more dangerous.

**Recommended approach:**

* 💥 Save for the Shotgun
* 🏃 Upgrade Speed
* 💨 Use Dash during difficult encounters
* 🎯 Keep moving rather than standing still
* 💰 Continue saving toward stronger weapons

> **Goal:** Balance mobility, damage, and economy.

---

## 🔴 Late Game — Levels 51–100

This is where survival becomes the priority.

**Recommended approach:**

* 🔥 Unlock the AK-47 or Laser
* 💥 Upgrade Power
* 🛡️ Use Shield strategically
* 💨 Keep Dash available for emergencies
* 🌀 Keep moving around the arena

> **Goal:** Survive increasingly powerful enemy waves and defeat every Boss.

---

# 👑 Boss Strategy

Bosses are slow but extremely durable.

### Recommended tactics

**1. Maintain medium range**
Don't let the Boss get too close.

**2. Keep moving**
Use circular movement to avoid attacks.

**3. Save Shield**
Activate Shield when your health becomes dangerously low.

**4. Save Dash**
Don't waste Dash unnecessarily. Keep it ready for emergency escapes.

**5. Keep attacking**
Use your strongest available weapon and maintain consistent damage.

### 🔄 Simple Boss Pattern

```text
Attack
  ↓
Move
  ↓
Dodge
  ↓
Attack
  ↓
Shield when needed
  ↓
Dash if trapped
  ↓
Repeat
```
