🎮 What You Get
Zombie Runner is a fully functional browser-based 3D wave-defense game featuring:

🔫 5 unique weapons — Pistol, Uzi, Shotgun, AK-47 & Laser
👾 5 enemy types — Dog, Skeleton, Goblin, Troll & Boss
📈 Upgradeable stats — Speed & Power, 5 levels each
⚡ Special abilities — Dash & Shield with cooldowns
🌊 100 levels of progressively increasing difficulty
🖥️ Professional UI with menus and settings
🌑 3D graphics with lighting and shadows
📦 Completely offline playable
🚫 No installation required
🚫 No account required

🎮 How to Play
Deploy into the zombie-infested road.
Survive 5 waves per level.
Kill zombies to earn Score, Kills, and Cash.
Use cash to buy weapons, Armor, and Shields.
The final wave contains a boss plus additional enemies.
Defeat the boss to clear the level, recover 30 HP, and continue to the next level.
⌨️ Game Keys
W A S D — Move
Mouse — Aim camera
Left Mouse Button — Fire
R — Reload
SHIFT — Sprint
TAB — Open/Close Armory
P — Pause/Resume
🔫 Combat & Weapons

You start with the Assault Rifle. Buy additional weapons in the Armory and they automatically equip after purchase. Weapons have different damage, fire rate, magazine size, range, and firing styles. Running out of ammo automatically starts a reload.

🧟 Enemy Types
Walker — Standard zombie
Stalker — Faster threat
Corrupted Wolf — Fast animal enemy
Brute — Heavy, stronger zombie
Boss / Colossus — Appears on the final wave and has a special area slam attack.
🛡️ Defense System
Armor: Costs 300 Cash, increases maximum HP, and reduces incoming damage. Maximum: 10 armor levels.
Shield: Costs 500 Cash, absorbs damage before your HP and has 20% of your maximum HP as shield health.
Both have a 30-second gameplay cooldown after use/purchase conditions.
🏆 Main Goal

Survive → Kill enemies → Earn Cash → Upgrade Gear → Clear 5 Waves → Defeat the Boss → Reach the Next Level.

Tip: Keep moving, don't let enemies surround you, reload before getting too close to a large group, and save cash for stronger weapons and defense upgrades.
